Supported Formats

📄 PDF

Perfect for documents, portfolios, and printing. Customizable orientation, size, and margins.

🖼️ JPG

Universal image format. Best for photos, social media, and maximum compatibility.

📷 PNG

Lossless format with transparency support. Ideal for screenshots and graphics.

🌐 WEBP

Modern web format with superior compression. Smaller file sizes while maintaining quality.

How to Use

1

Select Format & Upload Files

Open the extension by clicking on the application icon. Choose your desired output format (PDF, JPG, PNG, or WEBP) and upload your HEIC or HEIF files by clicking "Select Files" or dragging and dropping them into the upload area.

2

Customize Settings (Optional)

Adjust conversion settings:

  • For PDF: Set orientation (portrait/landscape), page size, fit mode, and margins
  • For Images: Adjust quality settings (0.1 to 1.0) for optimal file size and quality balance

Settings are saved automatically and persist between sessions.

3

Conversion Results

After conversion, you'll see the processed files page with all your converted files ready for download.

4

Download Files

Download your converted files:

  • Download ZIP: Get all files in a single ZIP archive
  • Individual files: Click the download icon on each file card to download separately

All processing happens locally in your browser - your files never leave your device.

Tips & Best Practices

100% Private & Offline - All conversion happens locally in your browser. Your files never leave your device.

Use Fullscreen Mode for Batch - For processing many files, use the fullscreen mode for better organization and preview.

PDF Settings - Customize PDF orientation, page size, and margins for professional document output.

Quality Settings - Adjust image quality (0.1-1.0) to balance file size and quality. Higher values = better quality but larger files.

No Sign-up Required - Just install and use. No account creation, no cloud uploads, no tracking.
